# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Email **info@almc.es** with subject `SECURITY` including:

- Affected product/repository and version
- Steps to reproduce or proof of concept
- Impact assessment

We acknowledge reports within 72 hours and keep you informed until resolution. We ask for coordinated disclosure: please give us reasonable time to ship a fix before publishing.

## Scope

Repositories under the `ALMC-SECURITY-SLU` organization and the production services of ALMC SECURITY S.L.U. Out of scope: denial-of-service, social engineering, physical attacks and third-party services.
